ABOUT MASCO PIPELINES

Who We Are

×
THE JV PARTNERS

Masco Pipelines is a newly established joint venture between MASCO Energy Company and CIS Gaz Arabia — two industry leaders with decades of complementary expertise in pipeline construction and energy infrastructure.

The company combines MASCO Energy’s deep roots in Saudi Arabia’s infrastructure landscape and alignment with Vision 2030 with CIS Gaz Arabia’s proven EPCC capabilities and international pipeline track record.

Headquartered in Al Khobar, Eastern Province — the heart of Saudi Arabia’s energy sector — Masco Pipelines is uniquely positioned to serve Aramco, SABIC, and the Kingdom’s expanding giga-project pipeline needs.
