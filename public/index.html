EPCC Services

Oil and gas pipelines, networks and stations — from engineering to commissioning.

  • Oil & gas transmission pipelines
  • Collection & distribution networks
  • Compression & pumping stations
  • Metering & pressure regulating stations
  • Cogeneration & solar power plants
02 / 04

Operations & Maintenance

Long-term asset care built around pipeline integrity and dependable operation.

  • Pipeline integrity management
  • Preventive & corrective maintenance
  • Inspection & condition monitoring
  • Emergency response & repair
  • Asset lifecycle optimization
03 / 04

Industrial Engineering

Engineering design, project management and specialist trenchless expertise.

  • Engineering design & FEED studies
  • Project management (PMC / EPCM)
  • Technical consultancy & advisory
  • HDD & trenchless crossings
  • Directional & horizontal drilling
04 / 04

Water & Energy Infrastructure

Integrated capabilities for transmission, treatment and energy infrastructure.

  • Main water transmission pipelines
  • Sewage & water treatment plants
  • Power substation construction
  • Renewable energy systems (solar)
  • Battery energy storage (BESS)
